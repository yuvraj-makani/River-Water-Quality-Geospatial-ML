## Satellite-Based Assessment of River Water Quality and Human Influence Using Geospatial ML
A geospatial remote sensing project focused on satellite-based analysis of the Ganga River corridor to investigate river water quality and surrounding human influence using Google Earth Engine, Python, and QGIS.

## Project Overview

This project was undertaken in the context of a summer internship at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee. It focuses on developing repeatable geospatial workflows for processing satellite imagery and analyzing land-use patterns and human influence across the Ganga River Basin.

The study covers the Ganga River corridor from Kanpur to Varanasi, considering a 150 km buffer zone on either side of the river. Satellite datasets and geospatial tools are used to support large-scale image processing and land-use analysis.

## Objectives

Develop automated satellite imagery processing workflows using Google Earth Engine.

Analyze land-use patterns along the Ganga River corridor.

Explore the relationship between surrounding human activities and river environments through geospatial analysis.

Integrate Python and QGIS into a repeatable geospatial processing pipeline.

Establish a foundation for satellite-based river water quality assessment and environmental monitoring.

## Study Area

Region: Ganga River corridor, Uttar Pradesh, India

Coverage: Kanpur to Varanasi

Buffer zone: 150 km on each side of the river

Domain: Remote sensing, geospatial analysis, land-use assessment, and environmental monitoring
